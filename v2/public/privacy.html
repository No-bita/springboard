Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Introduction

Collectr ("we", "our", or "us"), operated through crm.aaryanshah.co.in, provides an automated client communication and document collection workspace built for loan officers, chartered accountants, financial consultants, and operations professionals.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect your information and that of your clients when you access our software, connect third-party integrations (such as Gmail and WhatsApp), or submit documents through our platform.

By accessing or using Collectr, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only as necessary to provide our CRM workflow and document collection services:

2.1 User Account Information

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, business/firm name, and secure password hashes.
  • Billing & Credits: Recharge history, prepaid credit balance, and transaction identifiers.

2.2 Contacts & Case Records

  • Client Information: Contact names, mobile numbers, email addresses, loan or tax case tags, and notes entered by you.
  • Collection Requests: Checklists of requested documents, due dates, reminder cadences, and approval statuses.
  • Uploaded Files: Documents submitted by your clients (e.g., PAN cards, bank statements, ITR returns, identity proofs) stored in encrypted Cloudflare R2 object storage.

2.3 Google User Data (Gmail Integration)

When you explicitly choose to connect your Google account to Collectr using Google OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, we access your mailbox using the read-only scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). Specifically:

  • Email Metadata: Message IDs, thread IDs, timestamps (internalDate), sender and recipient email addresses, and subject lines.
  • Email Content: Email body text and snippets necessary to associate customer inquiries with their respective Contact workspace and active document requests.
  • Authentication Credentials: Secure, cryptographically encrypted OAuth refresh tokens. We never receive, see, or store your Google account password.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Collectr complies strictly with Google's user data policies for sensitive and restricted scopes:

Collectr's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No General AI Model Training: We do NOT use, transfer, or disclose Google Workspace or Gmail user data to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Data Brokering or Advertising: We do NOT sell, lease, or transfer Google user data to any third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.
  • Limited Human Access: No Collectr employees or contractors read your email messages unless: (1) you provide explicit, affirmative written consent for technical debugging; (2) it is strictly necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse); or (3) required by law.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to deliver, automate, and improve your collection workflow:

  • Omnichannel Synchronization: Display unified customer conversation timelines spanning WhatsApp interactions and synchronized Gmail messages within each Contact's Workspace.
  • Deterministic Reply Detection: Automatically classify inbound client emails matching active document requests, updating case statuses from "Waiting on Them" to "Needs Attention" without manual checking.
  • Automated Reminder Follow-Ups: Schedule and dispatch polite document reminders over WhatsApp and email until requested verification items are submitted.
  • File Organization: Verify uploaded document types, compute cryptographic hashes, and organize files into client-specific case folders.

4. Security, Encryption & Storage

We design our system with defense-in-depth principles to protect your sensitive records:

AES-GCM-256 Token Encryption

Google OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using Web Crypto AES-GCM-256 with key derivation. Plaintext tokens are never stored.

Strictly Ephemeral Access Tokens

Short-lived Google access tokens are generated just-in-time and kept only in V8 isolate memory. They are never written to disk or database.

TLS 1.3 In Transit

All communication with our edge servers, Google APIs, and WhatsApp endpoints is encrypted using TLS 1.3.

Tenant Data Isolation

All SQL queries against Cloudflare D1 are strictly scoped by user ID to prevent unauthorized cross-tenant data access.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain complete control over your data:

  • Disconnecting Gmail: You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time directly from the Collectr dashboard. Disconnecting immediately revokes the OAuth token with Google Identity and deletes all stored credentials from our database.
  • Google Account Permissions: You can also revoke Collectr's access directly via your Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Account & File Deletion: Upon account closure, all associated contacts, messages, activities, and uploaded files are permanently deleted from Cloudflare D1 and R2 within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted, enterprise-grade infrastructure providers to operate the service:

  • Cloudflare Inc.: Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare D1 database, and Cloudflare R2 object storage.
  • Google LLC: Gmail REST API (read-only integration) and Google Cloud Pub/Sub for real-time push notifications.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: WhatsApp Cloud Business API for automated template reminders and client messaging.

Each service provider is contractually bound to process data solely to execute their designated infrastructure duties.
